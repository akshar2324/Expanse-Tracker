Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AkSpend ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding any information collected through our Android mobile application.

Unlike traditional financial tools, AkSpend is an offline-first, client-side application. We put your financial privacy first by keeping your personal transactions and configurations strictly localized on your own physical mobile device.

2. Core Privacy Principle: Zero External Servers

Guaranteed Offline Security: We do not run, maintain, or connect to any cloud-based database, tracking dashboard, or storage servers. Your record sheets, savings targets, categorized budgets, and parsed metrics stay within an encrypted, sandboxed database (Room/SQLite) built right inside your Android operating system.

3. High-Risk Permissions & Local Sensitive Data

To deliver modern automatons for expense tracking, our application requests access to certain system permissions. Here is how we handle them:

A. SMS Log Processing (SMS Permissions)

If you explicitly grant SMS permissions, AkSpend reads dynamic notifications and messages containing transaction updates from financial institutions (e.g., banks, credit cards, UPI alerts).

Important: All processing is 100% local. The SMS content is processed entirely inside your local device by our algorithm to parse dates, amounts, and descriptions. This raw SMS text is never uploaded to any analytics backend, network endpoint, or third-party service.

B. Local Notification System

We utilize notifications to alert you of approaching budgets, transaction reminders, and recurring ledger items. These alarms are queued, scheduled, and triggered strictly on the Android OS level.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Because your application carries zero background telemetry frameworks and does not send requests over the network, no third-party tracking, profiling networks, analytics agencies, or target advertisers ever receive data about your active budgets, account values, or transaction habits.

5. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

Since we do not host or back up your data on external clouds:

  • You hold sole ownership and control over your data ledger.
  • Clearing your application cache or deep-uninstalling AkSpend from your device will permanently erase all local transactions, budget metrics, and histories.
  • Loss of your physical device means the loss of your ledger state, as we cannot restore records remotely.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for all audiences and does not collect any data from children under the age of 13.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy over time to reflect any system improvements. Changes will be posted to this page with an updated modification date.
