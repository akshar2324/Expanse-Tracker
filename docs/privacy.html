Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. The short version

  • AkSpend has no account, no servers and no internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. It leaves only when you export or share it yourself.
  • There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • Reading payment notifications is optional and off until you allow it. Message text is never saved.

This policy explains what the AkSpend Android app stores, where, and what you can do about it. "We" means the developer of AkSpend.

2. What AkSpend stores on your phone

AkSpend keeps the following in its private app storage on your device:

  • Transactions, accounts and transfers, budgets, savings goals, debts, recurring payments and reconciliations.
  • CSV import settings, categories it learned from merchants you categorized, and saved Activity filters.
  • App settings, such as your currency region and theme.
  • Your app lock passcode, stored only as a salted one-way hash, and the count of wrong tries.
  • If you turn on encrypted automatic backups, the backup password, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.

Android keeps this storage private to AkSpend; other apps can't read it. On phones with a screen lock, Android's device encryption also protects it. AkSpend opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so this data is not copied to Google Drive.

We never receive any of this data. AkSpend has no internet permission, so it has no way to send it.

3. Bank SMS and payment notifications

A. Pasting or sharing a bank SMS

You can paste a bank SMS into AkSpend or share one to it from your messages app. AkSpend reads only the text you give it, on your phone, and fills in a transaction for you to check before saving. AkSpend does not have SMS permission and cannot read your inbox.

B. Auto-import from notifications (optional)

If you choose to turn on auto-import, Android asks you to give AkSpend notification access. With it, AkSpend reads new notifications from a built-in list of messaging, bank and UPI payment apps, ignores every other app, and looks for payment messages on your phone.

  • Saved: amount, whether money came in or went out, the merchant or description, date, a suggested category, the last digits of the account when the message shows them, the app the message came from, and a one-way fingerprint used to skip duplicates.
  • Not saved: the message text, sender details, and any notification that isn't a payment, such as personal chats and OTPs.
  • Nothing is added to your records until you review it. Items you don't review are deleted after 60 days.
  • You can pause auto-import in the app, or remove notification access at any time in Android Settings › Notifications › Device & app notifications.

4. Permissions

  • Notification access (optional): only for auto-import, as described above.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face if you turn that on. Android handles the biometric check; AkSpend never sees your fingerprint or face data.
  • Run at startup, keep awake, and foreground service: these come with Android's WorkManager library and let AkSpend run scheduled work, such as automatic backups, on your phone.
  • View network state: also added by WorkManager. AkSpend has no internet permission and never connects to the network.

AkSpend does not ask for internet, SMS, contacts, location, camera, microphone or file-wide storage access.

5. Backups, exports and sharing

  • You can export a JSON backup, a database file or a CSV report. Android asks where to save it, or which app to share it with. Once shared, that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Automatic backups are off unless you turn them on. They are written only to a folder you pick. You can protect them with a password (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). If the folder you pick syncs to a cloud service, that service receives the file under its own terms.
  • Restoring a backup reads only the file you choose, and checks it before replacing anything.

6. Home screen widgets

The budget widget shows this period's spending on your home screen. While the app lock is on, it hides all amounts.

7. Third parties

AkSpend contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code, and shares data with no one. Google Play may collect information about app installs under Google's own privacy policy.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete any entry in the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling AkSpend, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes everything it stored.
  • Backups and exports you saved stay wherever you put them until you delete them.
  • We hold no copy, so we cannot recover data for you after you lose your phone. Keep a backup if that matters to you.

9. Children

AkSpend is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If AkSpend changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
