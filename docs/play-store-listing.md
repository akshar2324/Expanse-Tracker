# AkSpend Play Store Listing Copy

## Short Description
Track your spending offline. Fast, private, and simple personal finance tracker.

## Full Description
AkSpend is a private, offline-first personal finance app designed to help you track your daily spending, manage accounts, and stay on top of your budget without relying on cloud sync or backend services. Your financial data stays entirely on your device, giving you full control over your privacy.

Built with simplicity in mind, AkSpend makes it easy to log expenses on the go, manage multiple accounts, set savings goals, and understand your financial habits.

## Features
- **Offline First**: No account required, no cloud sync, and no internet connection needed for core features.
- **Track Everything**: Easily log income, expenses, and transfers with category, date, and payment method details.
- **Account Management**: Keep track of cash, bank accounts, wallets, savings, and credit cards in one place.
- **Budgets & Goals**: Set monthly or weekly budgets and monitor your progress towards savings goals.
- **Import & Export**: Seamlessly import bank statements from CSV files, and export your local data via JSON backups when needed.
- **Privacy First**: Secure your app with biometric authentication.
- **Quick-Add Widget**: Log transactions instantly right from your home screen.
- **Ad-Free**: A completely clean experience with no ads or tracking.

## Privacy & Offline-First Wording
Your financial data is personal, and we believe it should stay that way. AkSpend stores all your core finance data locally on your device in a private database. We do not require an account, we do not sync your data to the cloud, and we do not collect telemetry or analytics. Backup and export actions only happen when you explicitly choose to perform them.

## Content Rating & Data Safety Notes (Manual Verification)
When filling out the Google Play Console, please manually verify the following:
- **Data Collection**: AkSpend does not collect, share, or transmit personal or financial data off the device.
- **Data Sharing**: No data is shared with third parties.
- **Account Creation**: No account creation is required to use the app.
- **Permissions**: The app only uses permissions strictly necessary for local functionality (e.g., biometric prompt). It does not request internet access for data transmission.
- **Content Rating**: Suitable for all ages. Does not contain sensitive content, ads, or restricted materials.
