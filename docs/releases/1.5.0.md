# AkSpend 1.5.0

A private, offline expense tracker for Android. No account, no cloud, no ads.

## What's new in 1.5.0

**Import from your bank**
- **Bank SMS import.** Paste or share a bank message and AkSpend fills in the amount, merchant, date and account for you to check before saving. One-time passwords are ignored, and the message text is never saved.
- **Auto-import (optional).** Payment notifications from messaging, bank and UPI apps wait in a review list until you confirm them.
- **CSV statements.** Import with a staged preview, and duplicates are flagged so nothing is added twice.
- **Category memory.** AkSpend remembers the category you chose for a merchant and suggests it next time.

**Plan ahead**
- **Month-end forecast.** See your projected balance, with a breakdown of bills and expected spending.
- **Subscription finder.** Spots repeating payments so you can turn them into reminders.
- **Better Activity search.** Filter by type, account, amount and date range, and save the filters you use often.
- **Budget widget.** See what's left this month on your home screen.

**Private and safe**
- **Stronger app lock.** Your passcode is stored as a salted hash, wrong tries are limited, and the app relocks after 30 seconds away.
- **Automatic backups (optional).** Back up to a folder you choose, optionally encrypted with a password.
- **Safer restores.** Backups are checked before a restore replaces anything.

**Look and feel**
- **Atelier**, a new default style with ivory and ink tones and serif numbers. Liquid glass is still available in More › Appearance.
- Clearer wording, real empty states and safer deletes throughout.

**Fixes**
- Import CSV no longer crashes when opened.
- A draft transaction survives rotating the phone.
- Large text sizes no longer clip key amounts on the add screen.
- Money parsing, transfers, and backup and restore edge cases are fixed.

## Install (APK)

1. On your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer), download **AkSpend-1.5.0.apk** below.
2. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser for this one install.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. No sign-up needed.

The app asks for no internet permission. You can check this in [AndroidManifest.xml](https://github.com/akshar2324/Expanse-Tracker/blob/main/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml) or with any APK inspector. Only download AkSpend from this releases page or Google Play.
