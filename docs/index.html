How it works

Log it, see it, stay on budget.

  1. 1

    Log a spend in two taps

    Add it in the app or from the home-screen widget. Pick the account it came from; moving money between your own accounts never counts as income.

  2. 2

    See where it went

    Insights shows spending by category, the last seven days and a month-end forecast, all worked out on the phone.

  3. 3

    Stay under budget

    Set weekly or monthly limits per category, watch them fill, and get a heads-up before you go over.

Features

What else is inside

  • Many accounts, one total

    Cash, bank, wallet, savings and credit cards. Transfers move money between them without counting as income or spending, and reconciliation keeps balances true.

  • Bring your statements

    Import bank or wallet CSVs in a staged review. Duplicates are caught by row fingerprint.

  • Biometric lock

    Fingerprint or face unlock, backed by the Android keystore.

  • Backups you hold

    Export a JSON backup or the raw database and restore it whenever you like. Android cloud backup is switched off.

  • A weekly digest

    Top categories, unusual spends and a month-end forecast, plus savings goals alongside your budgets.

  • Recurring, loans and a widget

    Recurring transactions, money you've lent or borrowed, a quick-add home-screen widget, and light and dark themes that follow your phone.

Privacy

Many money apps earn from your data. AkSpend never sees it.

There is no AkSpend server. Your ledger is a database on your phone, and it only leaves when you export it yourself.

The source code is public, and the app's manifest asks for no internet permission. Read the privacy policy.

Become a tester

AkSpend is getting ready for Google Play. Help test it for 14 days before launch. Joining takes about two minutes, and it's free.

  1. 1

    Join the Google Group

    Open testers-community and tap Join group.

  2. 2

    Use your Play account

    Join with the same Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your phone.

  3. 3

    Open AkSpend on Play

    On your phone, open the Google Play listing. Not there yet? Wait a few minutes after joining and try again.

  4. 4

    Install and keep it

    Install it like any app and keep it on your phone for 14 days. Updates arrive through Play.

Installing takes about a minute

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    On your Android phone, tap Download APK. It opens the latest release on GitHub. Tap the file ending in .apk.

  2. 2

    Allow this one install

    Android asks before installing apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.

  3. 3

    Install and open

    Tap Install, then Open. There's no sign-up: you're logging your first expense straight away. You can turn the browser permission off again afterwards.

Is it safe? You can check.

  • No internet permission. An app's manifest lists what it's allowed to do, and AkSpend's has no internet access, so it can't send your data anywhere. Check it in the source or with any APK inspector app.
  • Built from public code. Every release is built from the open-source repository, so anyone can read what it does.
  • Only download from this page or the GitHub releases page. Copies of the APK on other sites aren't ours.
