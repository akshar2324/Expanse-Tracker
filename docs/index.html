Core Capabilities

An executive personal ledger that keeps you in command of your money

AkSpend does of all the heavy calculations, indexing, and budget monitoring locally inside your secure Android sandbox. No accounts, no sign-ups, no data harvesting.

💳

Multi-Currency Local Ledger

Create instant transaction records by switching local currency settings effortlessly (supports ₹, $, €, £, and custom symbols). Formats figures on the fly based on current systems, maintaining your correct cumulative and transactional balance state perfectly.

📊

Intelligent Expense Tracking

Easily categorize and log your daily expenses. AkSpend maintains a clean ledger of your spending habits, helping you visualize where your money goes without ever touching the cloud.

⏱️

Categorized Monthly Budgets

Establish custom fiscal caps for individual pillars like Foods, Shopping, Transport, and Bills. The application constantly assesses your real-time spend velocities against limits, serving subtle progress graphics and alerts if you approach limits.

🎯

Savings Goals & Target Milestones

Set up dedicated buckets for emergencies, modern tech setups, or travel funds. Record compound savings, assign targeted resolution dates, and distribute liquid cash to goals to observe completion rates climbing towards 100%.

🔄

Recurring Bills Schedule

Stay on top of repeating overheads such as insurance, software subscriptions, or home rent. Pre-configure items with daily, weekly, or monthly triggers, and AkSpend's scheduler will queue and generate correct ledger transactions automatically.

🎨

Material 3 Obsidian Canvas

An interface designed to keep you concentrated. Implements a fluid screen setup featuring custom-derived state transitions, detailed dark theme layouts, adaptive spacing columns, and gorgeous custom canvas-bound bar and cash flow vectors.

The Zero-Server Manifesto

Designed to protect your financial footprint from day one

Your budget is your personal business. Traditional companies log your habits on their databases to advertise and optimize their profits. AkSpend is designed under an opposite, uncompromising local-first design:

  • ✓ No Cloud Accounts: You don't need a username, phone linking, password reset portal, or identity login screen. You install the app, set basic parameters, and start logging instantly.
  • ✓ Fully Isolated Room Database: All data cells sit within SQLite tables encrypted by standard Android system-level sandboxing.
  • ✓ Raw Local SMS Scans: Our parser translates transaction strings inside device runtime memory. The original SMS files never get parsed onto server APIs.
  • ✓ No Background Trackers: We deploy zero Facebook SDKs, Google Analytics libraries, Mixpanel codes, or third-party marketing tags inside our builds.
