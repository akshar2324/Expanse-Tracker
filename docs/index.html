    Features

    What else is inside

    • Finds forgotten subscriptions

      The subscription finder spots payments that repeat on a schedule, so you can turn them into reminders and see them in your forecast.

    • Budgets with a heads-up

      Set weekly or monthly limits per category, watch them fill, and get a heads-up before you go over. Savings goals sit alongside your budgets.

    • Many accounts, one total

      Cash, bank, wallet, savings and credit cards. Transfers move money between them without counting as income or spending, and reconciliation keeps balances true.

    • Bring your statements

      Import bank or wallet CSVs in a staged review. Duplicates are caught by row fingerprint.

    • Find any transaction

      Search by description, category or payment method. Filter by type, account, amount and date range, and save the filters you use often.

    • A weekly digest

      Top categories, unusual spends and a month-end forecast. Insights shows spending by category and the last seven days, all worked out on the phone.

    • Biometric lock

      Fingerprint or face unlock, backed by the Android keystore.

    • Backups you hold

      Export a JSON backup or the raw database and restore it whenever you like. Android cloud backup is switched off.

    • Recurring, loans and a widget

      Recurring transactions, money you've lent or borrowed, a quick-add home-screen widget, and light and dark themes that follow your phone.

    Privacy

    Many money apps earn from your data. AkSpend never sees it.

    There is no AkSpend server. Your ledger is a database on your phone, and it only leaves when you export it yourself.

    The source code is public, and the app's manifest asks for no internet permission. Read the privacy policy.

    What it can and can't reach

    • Your SMS inboxNever asked

    Is it for you?

    An honest answer before you install.

    You'll like it if

    • You pay by UPI or card and want to see where it all went.
    • You won't hand a money app your bank login or your data.
    • You want budgets, savings goals and a record of money lent or borrowed in one place.
    • You'd like your records in a backup file that you keep.

    Look elsewhere if

    • You want it to log in to your bank and sync by itself. AkSpend doesn't connect to your bank or move money.
    • You want live sync between several phones. There's no cloud; you move data with a backup.
    • You need stocks or mutual funds tracked.
    • You're on iPhone. It's Android only.

    Installing takes about a minute

    1. 1

      Download the APK

      On your Android phone, tap Download APK. It opens the latest release on GitHub. Tap the file ending in .apk.

    2. 2

      Allow this one install

      Android asks before installing apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.

    3. 3

      Install and open

      Tap Install, then Open. There's no sign-up: you're logging your first expense straight away. You can turn the browser permission off again afterwards.
