# AkSpend Tester Onboarding

Welcome! Thank you for helping test AkSpend, an offline-first Android personal finance tracker. This guide will walk you through how to join the closed test, what kind of feedback we are looking for, and how to reach out to other testers.

## How to Join the Google Play Closed Test

Joining takes about two minutes:

1. **Join the Google Group**: Open [testers-community](https://groups.google.com/g/testers-community) and tap **Join group**.
2. **Use your Play account**: Join with the same Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your phone.
3. **Open AkSpend on Google Play**: On your phone, open the [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akshar.akspend). If it isn't visible yet, wait a few minutes after joining and try again.
4. **Install and keep it**: Install AkSpend like any app and keep it installed for 14 days. Updates arrive through Google Play.

## What Feedback to Provide

Your insights are invaluable. While testing AkSpend, please pay attention to:

- **Bugs and Crashes**: Did the app crash? Did a specific action fail? Please describe what you were doing when it happened.
- **Usability**: Is the app easy to navigate? Are the budgeting or transaction entry workflows intuitive?
- **Feature Requests**: What features do you feel are missing that would make the app better?
- **Performance**: Does the app feel fast and responsive?
- **Data Import/Export**: Try importing a CSV file or taking a backup. Did it work as expected?

Please send feedback by [opening an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/akshar2324/Expanse-Tracker/issues).

## Privacy Note

**Your finance data stays local.**
AkSpend is designed with privacy as the highest priority. All your transaction data, accounts, and budgets are stored locally on your device. The app does not sync your financial data to the cloud, nor does it send any analytics or telemetry to us.

*Note: Any feedback or screenshots you provide should be sanitized. Please do not share real, sensitive personal financial information in your feedback reports.*
